# Fajita logo system

Version 1.0 · Phase 1

## The mark: the held pulse

A heat spike held inside a controlled boundary, with the ember dot lifting off the peak. One shape tells the whole story: heat (the spike), control (the boundary), detection (the dot; the moment Fajita notices). The mark is original vector work drawn on a 64-unit grid in this repository. No stock marks, generators, or third-party icons were used.

The ember dot is the system's connective tissue: it crowns the mark, replaces the tittle of the j in the wordmark, and pulses as the observer in the Thermal Stack.

The boundary's corner radius (16/64 of the frame) is promoted to the signature brand radius token (`--radius-lg`), so brand frames and demo frames literally share the logo's geometry.

## The wordmark

"Fajita" set in the brand display instance of Fraunces (variable, wght 600, opsz 144, SOFT 0, WONK 0), converted to outlines by `scripts/generate-wordmark.ts` and stored as path data (`wordmark-data.json`). The j is set dotless and its tittle is replaced by the ember dot at the tittle's exact measured position. Fraunces is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits this use.

The wordmark is always rendered from the baked outlines, never re-set live in the font. Rendering the name in running text stays plain: "Fajita."

## Territory exploration (provenance)

Six conceptually distinct territories were designed and evaluated before selection. Sketches are preserved in `src/components/brand/logo/logo-territories.tsx` and displayed in the Brand Lab. Summary of the evaluation:

| # | Territory | Small size | Motion | Product UI | Restaurant risk | Distinctiveness | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Thermal trace | Fails at 16px | Strong | Weak (reads as chart) | None | Medium | Rejected |
| 2 | Signal F | Good | Medium | Good | None | Low (crowded F-mark territory) | Rejected |
| 3 | Monitored burner | Good | Medium | Good | High (stove pictogram) | Low (radar/target cliché) | Rejected |
| 4 | The held pulse | Strong | Strong (dot = heartbeat) | Strong | None | High | **Selected** |
| 5 | Skillet topology | Fails (frying pan at small sizes) | Medium | Weak | Very high | High | Rejected |
| 6 | Cool to hot | Good | Weak | Medium | None | Very low (equalizer icon) | Rejected |

Selection criteria were documented before scoring: 16px survival, monochrome survival, motion potential, product-UI suitability, status-page suitability, avatar crop survival, distance from restaurant branding, distance from competitor marks.

## Components

`FajitaLogo`, `FajitaMark`, `FajitaWordmark`, `FajitaPoweredBy` (in `src/components/brand/`). Controlled props: `tone` (auto, light, dark, mono), `orientation`, `size`, `animated`, `label`. There is deliberately no free color prop, no width/height distortion path, and no way to restyle the wordmark in another font.

## Rules

**Clearspace:** the boundary corner radius (25% of mark height) on all sides. **Minimum sizes:** mark 16px, horizontal lockup 96px wide, wordmark 60px wide.

**Forbidden:**

- Stretching, skewing, rotating, or outlining any variant
- Recoloring outside the defined tones; gradients or shadows on the mark
- Re-setting the wordmark in any font, or typing "Fajita" as a logo substitute
- Placing the light-ink variants on light backgrounds (and vice versa)
- Separating the ember dot for decorative use outside the defined system (mark, wordmark j, Thermal Stack observer)
- Any addition of flames, peppers, sombreros, skillets, or food imagery

**Status-specific treatments:** the mark's ember dot may adopt status colors only inside the product (e.g. app loading states), never on marketing surfaces or the customer status page.

**"Powered by Pamphlet" compatibility:** when a surface carries both attributions, place the two lockups on one row with at least 24px separation, equal visual weight, Fajita's lockup using the quiet powered-by treatment. Never merge them into one phrase or share one icon.

## Accessibility

Every component takes a `label` (default "Fajita") rendered as `role="img"` + `aria-label`. Passing an empty label marks the SVG decorative (`aria-hidden`), used when the mark sits beside the wordmark. The animated dot respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Regeneration

```bash
# wordmark outlines (requires .brand-src/Fraunces-var.ttf, gitignored)
npx tsx scripts/generate-wordmark.ts
# OG tagline outlines (requires .brand-src/InstrumentSans-var.ttf)
npx tsx scripts/generate-tagline.ts
# static /public/brand exports
npx tsx scripts/export-brand-assets.ts
```

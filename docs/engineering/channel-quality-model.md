# Channel quality model

Scorecards include visitors, qualified visitors, signup, paid, activated, Day-7/30 retained, MRR, retained MRR, refunds, chargebacks, support, abuse, cost, activated CAC, retained CAC, payback, contribution, confidence, completeness.

States: researching → preparing → limited_test → validating → repeatable → scaling → holding | paused | rejected | retired.

Do not hide poor retention behind low CAC.
